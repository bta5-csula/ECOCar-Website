Inclusive access

Accessibility

Cal State LA EcoCAR is committed to providing website content that can be used by people with a wide range of abilities and technologies.

Last updated October 25, 2026

Our standard

The team’s goal is to maintain this website in alignment with the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines, WCAG 2.1 Level AA, and Cal State LA’s digital-accessibility expectations.

Accessibility is considered when the team adds content, motion, images, links, video, and interactive elements.

Current approach

  • Use semantic headings, descriptive links, and alternative text for meaningful images.
  • Support keyboard navigation and visible keyboard focus.
  • Respect reduced-motion preferences for animated experiences.
  • Maintain readable contrast and avoid using color as the only source of meaning.
  • Review video for captions or an equivalent transcript when audio communicates meaningful information.

Report a barrier

If you have difficulty using any part of this website, email ecocarchargingeagles@gmail.com. Please include the page address and a description of the problem. You may also include your preferred way to receive the information.

The team will review reported barriers and work toward an accessible alternative or correction.

Campus resources

Visit Cal State LA Accessibility for university-wide accessibility support, guidance, and barrier-reporting resources.
