Mission and vision

Learn by building

Students turn classroom knowledge into hands-on experience through the design, integration, and testing of an electrified vehicle.

Grow the mobility workforce

The program develops technical, leadership, and communication skills that prepare students for careers in transportation and electrification.

Engineer with public purpose

The team connects vehicle innovation with the needs of communities, public agencies, industry, and first responders.

Who we are

Cal State LA EcoCAR

Cal State LA EcoCAR, nicknamed the Charging Eagles, brings together students from engineering, computing, business, and communications. Cal State LA is a Hispanic-Serving Institution with a nationally recognized record of expanding opportunity. The Equality of Opportunity Project ranked Cal State LA No. 1 in the nation for upward mobility. The project involved researchers from Harvard, Stanford, and Brown and continues today as Opportunity Insights at Harvard. Students gain practical experience while contributing to the future of transportation.

The team is one of twenty North American universities participating in the four-year EcoCAR Innovation Challenge and the only university representing California.

Meet the team

Our foundation

Experience built over time

Cal State LA has decades of student vehicle-development experience through Solar Eagle, Formula SAE, Baja SAE, EcoCAR, and the Battery Workforce Challenge.

That work established a foundation in battery engineering, embedded controls, vehicle integration, thermal design, model-based development, teamwork, and public outreach.

Why it matters

Mobility with public purpose

The team is developing a Wildfire Command Vehicle concept that explores how an electrified Jeep Cherokee could support search-and-rescue and emergency-response work. Students are defining the concept through requirements, modeling, packaging, fabrication, testing, and conversations with affected communities and fire-service professionals.
