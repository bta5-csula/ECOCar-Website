Dr. David Blekhman

Faculty Advisor

Biography

Dr. David Blekhman is a Cal State LA EcoCAR Faculty Advisor and previously served as the lead advisor for EcoCAR 2 and EcoCAR 3 from 2011 to 2018. He is a professor of Sustainable Energy and Transportation and the founding technical director of the Hydrogen Research and Fueling Facility at Cal State LA. When commissioned in 2011, the station was the largest academic-campus facility in the world producing its own hydrogen via electrolysis. An internationally recognized expert in hydrogen transportation, Dr. Blekhman has received multiple major honors, including the 2025 Cal State LA President’s Distinguished Professor Award, the 2023 CSU-system-wide Wang Family Excellence Award for Outstanding Scholarship, the 2022 Cal State LA Outstanding Professor Award, and the 2019–20 Fulbright Distinguished Chair in Alternative Energy Technology at Chalmers University in Sweden. As PI and Co-PI, Dr. Blekhman has helped secure multi-million-dollar funding for hydrogen infrastructure and advanced-transportation projects from DOT, AQMD, NSF, DOE, CEC, CARB, ANL, and others. Dr. Blekhman earned his BS and MS degrees in Thermal Physics and Engineering from Saint Petersburg State Polytechnical University in Russia and a PhD in Mechanical Engineering from SUNY Buffalo.

Dr. Silvia Martin

Faculty Advisor

Biography

Dr. Silvia L. Martin is a Full Professor of Marketing at California State University, Los Angeles. She serves as the College of Business Principal Investigator for the EcoCAR Innovation Challenge, supporting student teams as they shape the future of mobility. Dr. Martin conducts high-impact research in marketing and international business. Her work has appeared in leading journals, including Journal of International Marketing, Journal of Business Research, International Business Review, and Psychology & Marketing. She serves on the editorial board of Psychology & Marketing and served on the editorial board of Journal of Business Research from 2017 to 2025. With more than 33 years of experience in higher education, Dr. Martin has taught in the United Kingdom at the University of Bath, Cardiff University, and Bristol Business School; in France at Institut Commercial de Nancy; in Italy at La Salle University study abroad programs; and at Tecnológico de Monterrey and Instituto Tecnológico Autónomo de México (ITAM).

Dr. Chris Bachman

Faculty Advisor

Biography

J. Chris Bachman is an Associate Professor and Interim Chair of the Mechanical Engineering Department at Cal State LA. He received his B.S. (2010) and M.S. (2012) from the University of California, Davis (UC Davis) and his Ph.D. (2017) from the Massachusetts Institute of Technology (MIT). During graduate school, Dr. Bachman researched a range of electrochemical systems from hydrogen fuel cells to solid-state lithium-ion batteries. During that time, he taught experimental methods, control systems, and mechanical design at UC Davis; physics and calculus at MIT; fluid mechanics and mechanical vibrations at the Wentworth Institute of Technology (WIT); and now teaches Introduction to Engineering and Technology, Introduction to Mechanical Design, Thermodynamics, and Senior Design at Cal State LA. Dr. Bachman is a Fulbright US Scholar, under which he taught at UABC Tijuana – Valle de las Palmas and advised the CUDA FSAE team. He currently researches lithium-ion batteries for extreme applications with the Jet Propulsion Lab, redox flow batteries for grid storage with the National Science Foundation, and manufacturing in space with the National Aeronautics and Space Administration. He also currently directs the engineering college’s fabrication space, known as the ECST Makerspace, and the Cal State LA Materials Characterization Lab, and advises both the Baja SAE off-road race team and the EcoCAR Innovation Challenge EV design team sponsored by the Department of Energy and Stellantis. Dr. Bachman is the Chair of the Society of Automotive Engineers in Southern California.
