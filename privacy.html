Website information

Privacy & Analytics

Cal State LA EcoCAR uses limited website analytics to understand how visitors find and use the site and to improve its content.

Last updated October 25, 2026

Analytics services

The site uses Google Analytics 4 and Vercel Web Analytics. These services help the team understand page views, traffic sources, general device and browser categories, and broad geographic trends.

Google Analytics may use cookies and pseudonymous identifiers to measure interactions. Vercel Web Analytics provides aggregated reporting without third-party cookies and does not associate standard page-view information with an individual visitor or IP address.

How information is used

  • Measure visits and traffic trends over time.
  • Understand which pages and outreach links are most useful.
  • Improve website performance, navigation, and content.

The team does not intentionally send names, email addresses, or other directly identifying information to either analytics service.

Your choices

You can limit cookies through your browser settings. Google also provides a Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.

Leaving the site or following an external link may subject you to the privacy practices of the destination website.

Questions

For questions about this website or its analytics, email ecocarchargingeagles@gmail.com.

For university-wide privacy information, review the Cal State LA Privacy Notice.
